What Is SEO

Why SEO Is Your Highest-ROI Marketing Channel

SEO is the practice of improving your website so search engines rank it higher when customers search for your services. For contractors, this means showing up when someone searches "HVAC repair near me" or "roofing contractor in [city]."

📈
Evergreen Traffic
Unlike paid ads that stop the moment you pause spending, SEO compounds over time. Top rankings deliver free clicks 24/7.
📍
Local Intent
72% of consumers who do a local search visit a store within 5 miles. Local SEO puts your business in front of ready-to-buy neighbors.
💰
Higher Closing Rates
SEO leads close at roughly 15% vs. 2% for cold outreach because the customer already expressed intent by searching.
🏆
Authority & Trust
Ranking above competitors signals expertise. Customers equate #1 results with being the best in the area.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our SEO services, timelines, and guarantees.

❓ How long does SEO take to work?
Most contractors see measurable movement in 30-60 days, with strong rankings typically achieved in 90-120 days. SEO compounds over time.
❓ Do you guarantee #1 rankings?
No ethical SEO agency can guarantee #1 because search engines control the algorithm. We guarantee measurable progress, transparent reporting, and a 90-day satisfaction policy.
❓ What makes contractor SEO different?
Contractors rely on local intent and service-area pages. Our playbook is built specifically for home services — from GBP optimization to city landing pages and review systems.
❓ Do I need a new website?
Not necessarily. We optimize existing sites in most cases. If your site is severely outdated, we may recommend a rebuild as part of the technical phase.
❓ How do you track results?
Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, call tracking, and rank tracking software. We tie organic traffic to leads and revenue so you see the real ROI.
❓ What if I cancel mid-campaign?
You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. All completed work — optimized pages, citations, schema — stays with your site.
❓ Is SEO better than Google Ads?
SEO and ads serve different purposes. SEO delivers free, compounding traffic over time. Ads deliver instant visibility while you build SEO. Most contractors use both.
❓ Do you work outside the US?
Our contractor SEO playbook is optimized for US markets. International SEO is available by request with regional specialists.
