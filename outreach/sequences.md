## Follow-up Automation Rules

### Response-Based Triggers
- **Positive response:** Schedule demo call within 24 hours
- **Request for info:** Send 1-pager + case study within 1 hour
- **Objection raised:** Send relevant case study within 2 hours
- **No response:** Continue sequence as scheduled
- **Negative response:** Move to nurture queue, re-contact in 90 days

### Time-Based Rules
- All outreach sent between 8am-5pm recipient local time
- Email follow-ups sent 2-3 days apart
- LinkedIn messages sent 4-5 days apart
- Max 5 touches per sequence before breakup
- Breakup messages sent after 30 days of no response

## A/B Testing Framework

Test variables:
- Subject lines: question vs. statement vs. number
- Personalization depth: specific review vs. general stat vs. local hook
- Message length: <100 words vs. 100-150 words vs. 150+ words
- CTA type: question vs. statement vs. benefit
- Send time: morning vs. afternoon vs. evening
- Follow-up timing: 2 days vs. 3 days vs. 5 days

Minimum sample size: 100 leads per variant  
Test duration: 14 days or until statistical significance
